This Privacy Policy describes how CampusZen Learning Solutions (“CampusZen”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles personal information in connection with www.campuszen.in, course enquiries, enrollment requests, and related training communications. Please read it before submitting personal information.

01Overview and scope

This policy applies to information you provide when browsing our website, contacting our team, enquiring about programs, registering interest in training, or communicating with us. It does not automatically apply to independently operated third-party services that have their own privacy policies.

02Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with CampusZen, the information involved may include:

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone number, and messages submitted through our website or other communication channels.
  • Enrollment details: the program you are interested in and information you choose to provide when requesting admission, mentorship, or training support.
  • Communication records: questions, feedback, and correspondence with our team.
  • Technical information: browser, device, IP address, and website usage information, if collected by our hosting, security, or analytics services.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is specifically required and an appropriate secure process has been provided.

03How we use information

We may use relevant information to respond to enquiries; provide course, enrollment, scheduling, and mentorship information; communicate about requested training services; administer and improve our website and programs; protect our systems; and meet applicable legal obligations. Where required, we will obtain consent for optional promotional communications.

04Sharing and disclosures

We may share information with service providers that support website hosting, communications, registration, or program delivery, subject to appropriate safeguards. We may also disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect rights, safety, or security. We do not authorize third parties to use information provided to us for unrelated purposes. Any actual third-party integrations and data-sharing arrangements should be reflected in this policy before publication.

05Cookies and analytics

Our website or services used to operate it may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functionality, performance, or analytics. Browser settings may allow you to manage or block cookies. Blocking certain cookies could affect website features. If non-essential analytics or advertising cookies are enabled, we will provide notices and choices as required by applicable law.

06Data security and retention

We aim to apply reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the information we handle. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to legal, operational, or dispute-resolution requirements, and then delete or de-identify it where appropriate.

07Your choices and privacy rights

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have submitted, request correction or deletion where applicable, or withdraw consent for optional communications. Some requests may be limited by applicable legal obligations or legitimate retention needs. To stop promotional emails, follow any unsubscribe instructions provided or email us directly.

08Children's privacy

Our services are primarily intended for learners seeking educational and career-development programs. If a minor seeks to enroll or submit personal information, involvement of a parent or legal guardian may be required under applicable law. If you believe a child's information was submitted without appropriate authorization, please contact us.

09External websites and services

Our website may link to third-party websites, learning platforms, or communication services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those independent services. Please review their privacy policies before sharing information with them.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, data practices, or applicable law. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Material changes may be communicated through additional notices where required.
