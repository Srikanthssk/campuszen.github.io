✦ What you'll learn

From Medical Scans to Actionable Insights.

Build practical skills in imaging formats, preprocessing, computer vision, neural networks and model interpretation.

Image Processing

Explore DICOM and NIfTI, clean images, enhance contrast and extract useful features.

Explainable Medical AI

Evaluate sensitivity and AUC, explore Grad-CAM and examine privacy, bias and validation.
