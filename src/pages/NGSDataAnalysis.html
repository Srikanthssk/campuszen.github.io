Core capabilities

Make Sense of Sequencing Data.

Learn the steps of a real NGS analysis workflow, from checking raw data quality to interpreting variants and gene expression.

01

Quality Control

Inspect sequencing reads, review QC reports and trim low-quality data.

02

Alignment & BAM

Map reads to reference genomes, then sort, index and inspect alignment files.

03

Variant Analysis

Call, filter and annotate SNPs and indels using standard genomics tools.

04

RNA-Seq Analysis

Generate count matrices, compare expression and explore enriched pathways.
